About Me

Level Designer / Environmental Experience Designer

Chris Wang

I build level spaces that turn story context into player-driven decisions.

My primary focus is level design, environmental storytelling, player agency, and spatial gameplay structure. I am especially interested in action, shooter, RPG, and immersive gameplay spaces where exploration, combat pressure, and narrative context work as one system.

I approach level design as gameplay architecture: a level should not only contain gameplay, but actively shape what the player notices, chooses, risks, and remembers.
