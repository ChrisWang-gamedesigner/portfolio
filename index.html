About

I approach level design as gameplay architecture: a level should not only contain gameplay, but actively shape what the player notices, chooses, risks, and remembers.

Spatial Clarity Readable routes, landmarks, sightlines, and progression signals.
Player Agency Choices that feel intentional, not decorative.
Environmental Storytelling Story conveyed through layout, props, tension, and spatial memory.
Encounter Flow Combat pressure and exploration rhythm built into space.
Iteration Blockout, testing, diagnosis, revision, and documented design decisions.

This section is designed for North American level-design recruiters: it explains how each project will be documented once content is filled in.

Design Intent

Spatial Plan
