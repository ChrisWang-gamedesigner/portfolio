Featured Project

FPS Tutorial Level — Information Creates Advantage

This is a first-level FPS prototype designed to teach players that gathering information, choosing good positions, and planning movement can create advantages in combat.

Design Goals

  • Teach basic movement, dash, cover, and shooting.
  • Encourage players to observe before entering combat.
  • Use glass floors, high platforms, and portals to reward information gathering.
  • Introduce optional exploration through a hidden portal room.
  • End with a combat test where players must act carefully under incomplete information.

Level Flow

  1. Start Area: Learn movement and basic navigation.
  2. First Combat Room: Learn dash and target priority under pressure.
  3. Glass Observation Room: Observe enemy positions before committing to combat.
  4. Portal Bonus Room: Reward curiosity and route planning.
  5. Final Combat Room: Test player awareness with visible threats and hidden melee enemies.

Core Design Idea

The main design idea of this level is: players who gather information can make better decisions.

Iteration Notes

The initial version introduced many mechanics at once. After reviewing the design, I reorganized the level around clearer learning goals: movement, combat pressure, observation, optional exploration, and final mastery.

Current Status

This project is currently in whitebox stage. The next step is to refine encounter pacing, improve enemy placement, and record a short gameplay walkthrough.
